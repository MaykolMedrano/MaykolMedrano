### About Me

Applied economist working at the intersection of causal inference, public-sector data, and research software.

- **Experience:** Economic analysis and public policy, including four years at Peru's Ministry of Education.
- **Education:** M.A. in Applied Economics, [Pontificia Universidad Católica de Chile](https://economia.uc.cl/), and B.A. in Economics from Universidad Nacional de San Antonio Abad del Cusco.
- **Current work:** Applied research using administrative and survey data, causal inference methods, and reproducible data workflows.
- **Research interests:** Economics of education, urban economics, causal inference, machine learning, and public-sector data systems.
